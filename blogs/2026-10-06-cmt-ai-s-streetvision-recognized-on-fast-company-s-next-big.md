Cambridge, MA — October 6, 2026 — CMT AI, the world’s largest telematics and AI company for safer mobility, today announced that it has been named an honoree in the North America category of Fast Company’s 2026 Next Big Things in Tech Awards. Now in its sixth year, the annual list recognizes breakthrough technologies with the potential to transform industries and shape the future. Fast Company specifically recognized CMT’s StreetVision, the first AI road safety platform to predict and prevent crashes.

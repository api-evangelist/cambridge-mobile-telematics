# Cambridge Mobile Telematics

> Cambridge Mobile Telematics (CMT) is the world's largest telematics service provider. Its AI-driven DriveWell Fusion platform fuses sensor data from smartphones, proprietary Tags, connected vehicles, and dashcams to power usage-based insurance, crash detection and assistance, distracted-driving programs, fleet safety, and public-sector road-safety initiatives. Developer access is via the gated DriveWell Mobile SDK (iOS/Android) and the DriveWell Portal.
